# UX decision lenses

- **Hierarchy:** Can people identify current state, primary decision, and secondary paths?
- **Comprehension:** Are concepts, labels, and consequences understandable without insider knowledge?
- **Usability:** Can the core task be completed efficiently, corrected, and resumed?
- **Accessibility:** Do structure, contrast, input, motion, language, and assistive semantics remain inclusive?
- **Trust:** Are provenance, risk, privacy, cost, and system limits visible at the decision point?
- **Interaction:** Are feedback, latency, state changes, and reversibility predictable?
- **Responsiveness:** Does priority survive narrow, wide, touch, keyboard, and variable-content conditions?
- **Content:** Does every message help orient, decide, act, verify, or recover?
- **Differentiation:** Is distinctiveness rooted in product truth rather than novelty alone?
- **Composition, grid, and spatial rhythm:** Do bounds, alignment, spacing, grouping, emphasis, and responsive transformation express semantic priority across the complete page or task?
- **Brand expression and visual identity:** Does a product-grounded grammar create recognition across visual and verbal dimensions without weakening semantics, evidence, accessibility, or platform expectations?
- **Shape and form language:** Do geometry and containers distinguish semantic families, states, and controls coherently without becoming the sole carrier of meaning?
- **Surface, depth, and material:** Do layers, boundaries, elevation, texture, and transient surfaces communicate hierarchy and behavior while preserving legibility, performance, and interaction truth?
- **Data visualization integrity:** Does each visualization answer a defined question with honest encoding, visible scope, uncertainty, provenance, accessible alternatives, and inspectable underlying evidence?
- **Visual state language:** Are lifecycle, interaction, availability, severity, freshness, and synchronization states named, redundantly encoded, and connected to valid transitions and recovery?
- **Motion art direction:** Does motion communicate hierarchy, causality, continuity, feedback, and brand character within bounded performance and reduced-motion constraints?
- **Verbal design, voice, and tone:** Do vocabulary, action language, claims, state messages, and tone remain precise, consistent, consequence-aware, accessible, and recoverable?
- **Document design system:** Does semantic structure, reading order, navigation, evidence, authority, accessibility, reflow, print, export, and revision state survive the delivered document format?
- **Image art direction:** Does every image have a truthful communication role, coherent visual grammar, responsible representation, accessible equivalent, durable provenance, and resilient derivatives?
- **Campaign design system:** Do proposition, message, proof, creative grammar, encounter sequence, channel adaptations, claims, accessibility, measurement, and governance form one accountable system?
- **Platform-native adaptation:** Are product invariants preserved while layout, navigation, controls, inputs, system capabilities, lifecycle, tokens, and accessibility adapt intentionally to each platform context?
- **Design tokens:** Are visual choices organized by semantic role, consistently mapped across modes and states, and evolved without silent breakage?
- **Typography:** Do semantic type roles, hierarchy, reading measure, data alignment, responsive behavior, localization, and user scaling preserve readability and meaning?
- **Color:** Do palette character, semantic roles, contrast, theme behavior, status, and data encoding preserve brand fit and meaning without relying on color alone?
- **Component architecture:** Do reusable elements have stable anatomy, bounded variants, explicit states, coherent interaction contracts, and defined responsive behavior?
- **Theming:** Do appearance, brand, accessibility, and density modes preserve semantic roles, component behavior, and predictable preference precedence?
- **Design-system governance:** Are ownership, contribution, review, publication, versioning, deprecation, migration, exceptions, and quality evidence explicit and accountable?
- **Adaptive layout:** Does semantic priority, state continuity, comparison context, and action hierarchy survive changes in width, device, pane structure, and information density?
- **Motion and feedback:** Do transitions and progress explain actual state, preserve task context, distinguish acknowledgement from completion, support recovery, and retain all information with reduced motion?
- **Direct manipulation:** Are object, affordance, mode, target, constraints, preview, precision, commitment, and recovery visible across pointer, touch, and keyboard input?
- **Keyboard and expert workflows:** Are commands, focus, selection, batch scope, shortcuts, recovery, and equivalent pointer or assistive paths visible and semantically consistent?
- **Interface language:** Do canonical object, actor, scope, state, and operation names remain precise and consistent across labels, actions, activity, help, and recovery?
- **Accessibility language:** Are preferences, alternative input, interruptions, recovery, and accommodations described by behavior and consequence without stigma, blame, or required disclosure?
- **Localization and content resilience:** Do language, region, formats, international identity, translation coverage, bidirectional flow, and culturally dependent meaning remain explicit and robust?
- **Design evaluation:** Is review tied to an exact artifact, task, criterion, evidence type, severity rationale, controlled comparison, verification method, and revision-bound decision?
- **Behavioral psychology:** Is the selected principle a falsifiable, context-bound hypothesis that preserves accurate understanding, autonomy, accessibility, refusal, correction, exit, recovery, and delayed-outcome evaluation?
- **Decision architecture:** Are material facts visible before commitment, alternatives comparably understandable, and review, refusal, correction, withdrawal, and recovery available without manufactured friction?
- **Cognitive load and progressive learning:** Is avoidable burden removed without hiding material information, memory externalized, guidance tied to real outcomes, and later independent use evaluated?
- **Behavioral ethics:** Does the complete intervention preserve autonomy, welfare, fairness, transparency, and durable control across repeated exposure, affected groups, safeguards, and enforceable stop conditions?
- **Perception, salience, and affordance:** Do grouping, similarity, contrast, anchors, signifiers, and attention cues reflect semantic priority and action truth without hiding alternatives or exploiting selective attention?
- **Choice, comparison, and decision integrity:** Can people understand and compare options, defaults, costs, timing, exclusions, and commitment without decoys, asymmetric framing, artificial fatigue, or manufactured loss pressure?
- **Mental models, comprehension, and transfer:** Does the design reveal an accurate system model, bridge relevant prior knowledge, explain unavoidable complexity, and support independent use beyond one guided completion?
- **Research bias and evidence validity:** Are sampling, observation, expectation, recall, analysis, uncertainty, contradictory evidence, and decision limits explicit enough to prevent a preferred conclusion from masquerading as valid research?
- **Motivation, progress, and temporal agency:** Do goals, progress, estimates, milestones, reminders, rewards, pause, stopping, and disengagement serve a user-chosen outcome without false progress, dependency, or pressure?
- **Memory, learning, and experience continuity:** Is avoidable recall externalized, interruption recovery preserved, deliberate retrieval limited to explicit learning, and remembered experience evaluated separately from the lived journey?
- **Feedback, feedforward, and temporal state:** Can people predict consequences and distinguish acknowledgement, acceptance, processing, verification, completion, staleness, failure, retry, undo, and recovery through accessible evidence?
- **Social influence and persuasion integrity:** Are reviews, popularity, authority, endorsements, scarcity, reciprocity, and norms authentic, contextualized, auditable, and incapable of turning belonging or prior action into obligation?
- **Attention and interruption:** Is attention treated as a user cost, with urgency and channel matched to consequence, response window, reversibility, context, accessibility, privacy, and user-controlled suppression?
- **Information foraging and findability:** Can people form and refine intent, predict destinations from information scent, inspect the query model, control suggestions, and recover from weak results without opaque steering?
- **Error prevention and recovery:** Does the design prevent avoidable mistakes, preserve valid work, explain correction accessibly, distinguish lifecycle states, and make consequential submission reviewable or reversible?
- **Quantitative evidence and uncertainty:** Are scope, definition, denominator, comparison, provenance, uncertainty, exploration limits, reproducibility, and correction visible enough to support an accurate conclusion?
- **Procedural fairness and service recovery:** Can affected people be heard, inspect status and evidence, receive specific reasons and proportional remedies, obtain independent review, and verify durable correction without retaliation or repeated burden?
- **Conversation grounding, turn-taking, and repair:** Are topic, referent, actor, audience, understanding, response obligation, repair, commitment, correction, and handoff explicit enough for the consequence?
- **Situation awareness and resilient control:** Can operators perceive, comprehend, and project relevant state while challenging evidence, controlling automation safely, coordinating roles, verifying recovery, and completing learning?
- **Accountability, separation of duties, and independent review:** Are incompatible duties separated, authority bounded, review substantively independent, authorization exact-object bound, and decision evidence sufficient without becoming surveillance?
- **Rule legibility, consistency, and exception governance:** Are policy semantics, evidence inputs, precedence, conflicts, tests, exceptions, decision paths, appeals, and corrective action explicit, bounded, and historically linked?
- **Contextual integrity and meaningful data agency:** Are actors, information, purpose, context, inference, recipients, persistence, rights, defaults, cross-context reuse, third-party effects, and correction or deletion propagation explicit and proportionate?
- **Experimental validity and exposure ethics:** Can the design answer its stated question without invalid inference or disproportionate exposure, and are assignment, evidence, uncertainty, harm, safeguards, withdrawal, redress, rollout, and delayed effects explicit?
- **Multimodal equivalence and sensory agency:** Can people obtain equivalent meaning, timing, structure, controls, status, and recovery through the sensory and interaction modes available to them?
- **Data continuity and convergence confidence:** Can people distinguish local, queued, synchronized, stale, conflicting, and failed state, inspect provenance, and recover without silent loss or false convergence?
- **Speech agency, recognition, and repair:** Are listening, recognition, interpretation, audience, retention, confidence, correction, confirmation, interruption, and equivalent non-speech paths explicit and controllable?
- **Capture integrity and evidentiary review:** Are acquisition, transformation, recognition, authenticity, binding, eligibility, and decision represented as separate source-linked claims with proportionate review and recovery?
- **Imagery Illustration Art Direction:** Assign every image one explicit interface role before selecting its style: identity, object preview, evidence, instruction, status, editorial content, atmosphere or decoration; define the claim it may support, the adjacent information that must carry meaning without it and whether it is interactive; and do not let one attractive asset ambiguously stand in for product state, proof, action and brand at once.
- **Representational Integrity Inclusive Imagery:** Classify an image by purpose in its exact context before styling or describing it: decorative imagery contributes no required information and should be suppressible; informative imagery needs an equivalent for its relevant meaning; functional imagery needs an action or destination name; complex imagery needs an adequate extended representation; identity and evidence imagery need context, provenance and higher scrutiny; and the same asset may require a different classification when its use changes.
- **Submission Agency Data Minimization Content Safety:** Justify the submission at the field and purpose level: ask only for information necessary to deliver the stated outcome, explain why a file is needed and whether another path can satisfy the requirement, avoid collecting whole documents when a smaller attestation or field is adequate, accept interoperable formats where feasible, and periodically review whether the content, metadata and retained copies remain relevant and necessary.
- **Feedback Validity Power Reputational Remedy:** Treat solicitation as a source of measurement bias: document who is asked, when, how often, after which outcomes and through which channel; make decline and later response possible; avoid prompting only satisfied people, gating criticism, conditioning incentives on sentiment or interrupting people before meaningful use; compare respondents with the eligible population; and interpret increased review volume as changed sampling until evidence shows otherwise.
- **Human Agency, Calibrated Reliance, and Automation Boundaries:** Are proposal, recommendation, decision, and action distinct, with authority, uncertainty, verification, refusal, intervention, correction, and remedy proportionate to consequence?
- **Explanation, Contestability, and Decision Support:** Is an explanation faithful to the actual evidence and process, meaningful to its recipient, explicit about limits, and connected to correction, qualified review, appeal, and remedy?
- **Record Provenance, Correction, and Contestability:** Can people inspect where a material record came from, how it changed, who relied on it, and how corrections propagate without erasing prior evidence?
- **Identity Assurance, Proportional Verification, Exclusion, and Recovery:** Does verification match the actual risk while preserving accessible alternatives, bounded evidence collection, understandable failure, and humane recovery?
- **Delegated Authorization, Scope, and Revocation:** Are delegator, delegate, purpose, objects, actions, duration, effective permissions, evidence, expiry, and revocation explicit and reviewable?
- **Iconography and Visual Language:** Does every symbol have one semantic role, an appropriate label and accessible name, coherent visual treatment, redundant state encoding, and audience-tested meaning?
- **Symbol Comprehension and Redundant Coding:** Can critical meaning survive color, language, sensory, cultural, and environmental differences through text, shape, position, semantics, and appropriate redundancy?
- **Spatial Context and Location Agency:** Are location source, accuracy, freshness, audience, persistence, uncertainty, tracking state, spatial consequence, and user control visible and proportionate?
- **Sustainable Engagement, Wellbeing, and Autonomy:** Does recurring engagement serve the user's chosen outcome without exploiting interruption, social pressure, variable reward, artificial urgency, or difficult exit?
- **Context Boundary, Identity, and Orientation:** Can people tell which organization, workspace, account, object, mode, and state they are acting within before a consequential action crosses a boundary?
- **Authorship, Provenance, and Content Integrity:** Are human and system contributions, source evidence, transformations, approvals, versions, publication authority, correction, and withdrawal distinguishable?
- **Scarce Capacity Allocation Commitment Fairness:** Select an allocation rule deliberately rather than defaulting to fastest-click wins: evaluate first-come, priority, lottery, batch, queue, invitation and provider-discretion models against demand, harm, urgency, gaming, bot access, connectivity, disability, work schedule and digital literacy; publish the rule and material exceptions; use identity or eligibility controls proportionately; and audit whether intermediaries, automation or privileged groups capture scarce capacity.
- **Remedy Parity Value Restoration Burden Allocation:** Assign burden according to cause and control: distinguish changed mind, customer damage, defect, misdescription, non-delivery, provider cancellation, delay and platform or payment error; place shipping, evidence, fees, repeated contact and timing risk on the party best able to prevent or verify the failure; reduce proof when the business already holds tracking or operational evidence; do not charge people to correct business-caused failure; and explain any retained amount as a proportionate, itemized consequence rather than a generic penalty.
- **Earned Value Legibility Breakage Loyalty Fairness:** Evaluate value as a user-understandable exchange contract, not a nominal score: distinguish units from money, status, eligibility and inventory; show the range or basis of usable value, material restrictions, fees and conversion variance; preserve the earning terms with the resulting value; and test whether a reasonable person can predict what effort produces and what the balance can actually obtain.
- **Transaction Finality Recipient Certainty Recoverable Payment Control:** Make the financial proposition complete before authorization: present amount paid, amount and currency received, exchange rate and validity, provider and third-party fees, taxes, funding source, rail, availability date, limits, protection and cancellation boundary in a readily understandable form; separate guaranteed values from estimates; preserve a durable receipt; and test comprehension rather than mere disclosure presence.
- **Promise Accuracy Custody Transparency Exception Agency:** Evaluate every fulfillment promise against its evidence and decision consequence: preserve the original promise, basis, scope and responsible party; distinguish contractual date, operational target, probabilistic estimate and aspirational speed; show a range or confidence when precision is not supportable; update promptly when evidence changes; and never erase prior commitments or reset the baseline to make performance look on time.
- **Reproductive Autonomy Maternal Safety Loss Sensitive Care:** Evaluate reproductive experiences for private, informed, and nondirective choice; sourced and time-bound claims; maternal safety through postpartum; qualified benefit-risk decisions; respectful consent and care; system-wide loss-sensitive behavior; longitudinal continuity; and unequal outcomes that engagement or average outcome measures can conceal.
- **Opportunity Fairness Applicant Agency Accountable Selection:** Evaluate job relatedness, equal access, accommodation, informed applicant agency, structured comparison, accountable automation, adverse-evidence correction, procedural closure, and opportunity distribution across every selection stage.
- **Housing Opportunity Tenant Agency Accountable Screening:** Evaluate opportunity visibility, listing and total-cost truth, housing-relevant screening, proportionate data and fee collection, accommodation, adverse-evidence correction, durable tenancy records, and unequal burden or displacement across the complete housing funnel.
- **Learner Agency Assessment Validity Credential Integrity:** Evaluate meaningful learner agency, equitable participation, construct-valid assessment, inspectable evidence and scoring, repair-oriented feedback, accountable AI, education-record rights, bounded credential claims, and learning outcomes beyond engagement.
- **Ingredient Integrity Dietary Agency Allergen Safety Nutrition Context:** Evaluate exact and current ingredient evidence, dietary semantics, cross-contact uncertainty, serving-based nutrition, responsible communication, constraint-preserving substitutions, recipient-specific handoff, incident response and outcomes beyond conversion.
- **Taxpayer Agency Calculation Transparency Procedural Rights Compliance Equity:** Evaluate exact obligation identity, reconstructable calculations, source evidence, informed declaration, submission authority, payment and refund state, notices, correction and appeal rights, accessible help, unequal burden and outcomes beyond completion or refund salience.
- **Financial Inclusion Account Transparency Funds Access Fair Restriction Resolution:** Evaluate understandable account value, equitable opening and sustained use, provider and protection legibility, evidence-backed funds access, transparent fees and interest, proportionate restrictions, rights-preserving errors, switching and closure, and unequal exclusion or harm.
- **Essential Service Access Billing Affordability Continuity Remedy:** Evaluate essential services as sustained basic capability: test inclusive access, total-price and bill reconstruction, affordability, outage evidence, proportional restrictions, responsible handoffs, switching continuity, remedy, and unequal service loss beyond acquisition or collection.
- **Procurement Integrity Spend Authority Supplier Access Value Accountability:** Evaluate procurement across need, lifecycle value, scoped authority, conflicts, fair supplier access, current evidence, accessibility, order-to-payment integrity, bounded exceptions, performance, remedy, and outcomes beyond speed or nominal savings.
