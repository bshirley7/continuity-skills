# Precision reference translation

Use this gate after generative exploration and before complete concept expansion. Its job is to prevent strong references and generated studies from collapsing into generic HTML patterns.

## 1. Capture one exact source at a time

Capture the same named source at wide and narrow viewports. Do not blend several products into a category reconstruction. If several references matter, reconstruct each separately before synthesis. Record one `source_id` across both captures. Build a copy-adaptation ledger naming the exact crop, proportion, type placement, rhythm, material, interaction, or transition to reproduce. Third-party captures and reconstructions remain private and non-shipping.

Classify the source before rebuilding it. Photographic studies must prove crop fidelity, media transition, and responsive cropping; diagrammatic studies must prove information density, relationship topology, and label hierarchy; editorial studies must prove reading order, typographic rhythm, and material texture; motion-led studies must prove timing, state transition, and static fallback; product-object studies must prove object salience, material detail, and responsive object behavior. Mixed studies must name and pass their dominant medium, cross-medium hierarchy, and responsive medium priority.

## 2. Reconstruct before interpreting

Privately rebuild the selected frame and its responsive behavior in HTML/CSS. Copy the selected mechanics deliberately and closely: match region topology, salience order, subject scale, type role, density, crop anchors, negative space, material behavior, motion or interaction, and responsive transformation. Creativity and originality are not goals during reconstruction; learning through accurate copying is.

Create a combined source/reconstruction comparison at both viewports. Complete at least two correction passes. Locate every mismatch on the comparison, apply a named correction, and finish with a passed comparison. The first three elements noticed must survive in the same order.

The reconstruction is a calibration instrument. If it cannot reproduce the reference's defining behavior at desktop and mobile widths, the team does not yet understand the mechanic well enough to adapt it. When that behavior depends on occlusion, rebuild registered background, live-type, and alpha foreground planes. When it depends on typographic silhouette, declare the reconstruction's full `runtime_source_files` closure, run the shipped browser probe against the passed desktop reconstruction, and bind `typography_render_probe` inside that exact reference study. The probe's source-bundle fingerprint must cover the HTML and every linked local script, style, and component source; later concepts use that exact evidence as their metric baseline. “Similar condensed font” is not a measurement.

## 3. Compile an executable design grammar

Record at least eight distinct constraints. Include composition, scale, negative space, typography, media, responsive transformation, and at least one motion or interaction constraint. Every rule binds source and reconstruction locations plus a numeric measurement and tolerance. Adjectives or author assertions are not measurements.

## 4. Build a literal project substitution

Replace source roles with project roles while freezing the validated grammar. Map every constraint from a located source role to a located Continuity replacement. This deliberately literal baseline is private and may still look recognizably close to the source; its purpose is to prove that project truth can inhabit the copied design language before creative divergence begins.

## 5. Diverge under a visible change budget

Create the candidate adaptation from the literal baseline. Change only one to four grammar dimensions at once. Record the before, after, rationale, and preserved constraints for every change. A generated or source-defining material carrier must continue organizing the composition rather than becoming a background for generic display copy.

Image generation and editing may derive project-specific media and alternate concept mechanics. Extracted system rules do not require discarding useful generated pixels; record whether generated work is retained, translated, or not applicable. Promotion and rights remain separate decisions.

Measure divergence across composition, color, typography, media role, responsive behavior, and interaction. Every set requires a `close-study` averaging 0.25–0.55 distance and a `far-study` averaging at least 0.55 with four dimensions at or above 0.5. This prevents both timid cosmetic variation and untraceable novelty.

## 6. Review independently before expansion

Present source, reconstruction, literal substitution, and controlled adaptation together at wide and narrow widths. A reviewer identity distinct from the preparer must inspect the combined pixels and resolve every finding. Review:

- whether the first three focal elements survived in order;
- whether the same spatial tension and typographic role survived;
- whether material remains the organizer instead of a backdrop;
- whether the reference lineage is identifiable within five seconds;
- whether project identity is clearly distinct;
- whether narrow behavior preserves the idea rather than merely shrinking it;
- whether type over variable imagery has dependable contrast.

Validate the hash-bound record with:

```bash
continuity design reference-validate --manifest <reference-translation.json>
```

Workflow v3 uses precision manifest schema v2 and remains in `developing-reference-translations` until this passes. The command advances only to `developing-concepts`; it does not select, approve, publish, or authorize implementation.

## 7. Recheck during slop review

Supply `translation_fidelity` to each slop manifest. The native gate also reports primary material demotion, typographic grammar substitution, flattened compositional depth, unverified font transfer, source hybridization, self-certified fidelity, incomplete mobile comparison, weak lineage, and unresolved variable-image contrast. Collapsed approved planes or typographic character are project drift and cannot be waived. Default-risk findings require disposition.
