# Promise accuracy, custody transparency, and exception agency

## Generalized principles

- Evaluate every fulfillment promise against its evidence and decision consequence: preserve the original promise, basis, scope and responsible party; distinguish contractual date, operational target, probabilistic estimate and aspirational speed; show a range or confidence when precision is not supportable; update promptly when evidence changes; and never erase prior commitments or reset the baseline to make performance look on time.
- Treat delay as a new decision, not passive status: identify affected item, revised definite date or honest uncertainty, cause at a useful level, current custody, payment state and available alternatives; obtain meaningful consent when required; preserve immediate cancellation and correct refund routes; avoid silence-as-consent where the promise has repeatedly or materially changed; and record notices and choices.
- Separate event, inference and prediction: a label is an intent record, a scan is an observed event, carrier possession is custody evidence, a map position may be delayed or modeled, an ETA is a forecast, and delivered is a claim whose proof varies; label source and time, preserve contradictory evidence, state uncertainty, and prevent one weak signal from being promoted into conclusive completion.
- Make custody a visible chain of responsibility: show when risk and physical control move among seller, marketplace, warehouse, authenticator, carrier, courier, pickup site, delegate and recipient; connect each transition to item scope, event, actor, time and evidence; retain one accountable route for the consumer even when operations span providers; and do not force the person to adjudicate private contracts between merchant and carrier.
- Govern live tracking as temporary operational data: collect and expose only the location precision needed for coordination, name its freshness and limits, prevent inference of worker or recipient routines, restrict sharing and retention, provide non-map equivalents, and end active visibility when the operational purpose ends; never repurpose delivery movement for unrelated profiling without a separate legitimate basis and control.
- Make status accessible without multiplying interruption: expose text, sequence, actor, time and next action programmatically; do not rely on color, animation, sound or map position; let people choose operational channels and quiet behavior while preserving essential safety and handoff notices; retain status in a durable center; and keep help consistent across the complete process.
- Calibrate completion evidence to harm: use stronger recipient, signature, code, locker or condition evidence for sensitive, regulated, perishable or high-value handoff; minimize exposed address, image and bystander data; state what proof does and does not establish; support timely counterevidence and human review; and never deny a plausible missing, damaged or unsafe-delivery report solely because an automated event says delivered.
- Audit promise and exception systems across all affected people: measure original versus actual time, estimate error and calibration, update frequency, abandonment, cancellation access, split and partial outcomes, failed attempts, disputed delivery, remedy time, notification burden, privacy exposure and support handoffs; segment by disability, language, geography, housing, work schedule, device access and worker conditions; and avoid improving customer ETA optics by imposing unsafe or unachievable worker pressure.

## Method

- Inventory promise types, status sources, custody parties, evidence and remedies.
- Trace representative items through allocation, preparation, carrier handoff, delivery or pickup, proof and exception.
- Compare displayed estimate precision with historical calibration and update behavior.
- Test contradictions among merchant, carrier, map, recipient and proof signals.
- Review location, notification, image, signature and identity data by purpose and retention.
- Audit delay, abandonment, challenge, remedy and unequal burden for recipient and worker groups.

## Ethical safeguards

- Do not rewrite the original promise when estimates deteriorate.
- Do not treat repeated silence as consent to indefinite delay.
- Do not present a label, map marker or forecast as observed custody or completion.
- Do not send consumers through a merchant-carrier-platform responsibility loop.
- Do not retain or repurpose live courier or recipient location beyond operational need.
- Do not make status dependent on color, animation, sound or map use.
- Do not treat a weak automated delivery event as conclusive against counterevidence.
- Do not improve ETA appearance through hidden unsafe pressure on workers.

## Variation levers

- Use broader honest windows when forecast variance is high.
- Require stronger proof and privacy controls as item sensitivity or value rises.
- Increase temperature, allergen and condition evidence for food and medicine.
- Increase accessibility, delegation and loading support for pickup and bulky goods.
- Reduce live-location detail when a stage update is sufficient.

## Tensions and tradeoffs

- Precise estimates aid planning while increasing false certainty.
- Frequent updates reduce ambiguity while increasing anxiety and interruption.
- Proof protects against disputes while collecting sensitive data.
- Live maps support handoff while enabling surveillance and worker pressure.
- Centralized platform responsibility simplifies recovery while obscuring operational source detail.
- Strong identity checks secure delivery while excluding delegates or inaccessible users.

## Failure modes

- The current estimate replaces all evidence of the missed promise.
- Delay requires no active choice and quietly extends forever.
- A progress bar advances despite no custody event.
- The consumer must resolve conflicting merchant and carrier claims alone.
- Worker location remains visible after delivery.
- A map-only status is inaccessible.
- A delivery photo exposes private space yet does not prove recipient receipt.
- ETA targets improve while failed attempts, safety and remedy times worsen.

## Anti-patterns

- Promise-baseline laundering.
- Consent by endless waiting.
- Forecast-as-fact.
- Custody responsibility maze.
- Permanent last-mile surveillance.
- Map-only fulfillment.
- Proof overcollection.
- On-time optics metric.

## Acceptance and review questions

- Is every promise labeled by basis, scope, confidence, responsible party and original baseline?
- Does a delay create an understandable, recorded choice with cancellation or remedy rather than passive extension?
- Are observed events, inferred state, forecasts and completion claims visibly distinct?
- Can people trace item-level custody and responsibility without adjudicating provider contracts?
- Is live location purpose-limited, freshness-labeled, minimized, controllable and removed when no longer needed?
- Are status and essential updates accessible, durable, channel-controlled and non-map dependent?
- Is completion evidence proportionate, privacy-preserving, bounded in meaning and open to counterevidence?
- Are calibration, delay, exceptions, privacy, remedy, accessibility and worker effects audited together?
