# Customer, lead, qualification, opportunity, proposal, close, and handoff lifecycle

## Generalized principles

- Preserve one versioned relationship lineage while keeping person, contact point, organization, lead, opportunity, proposal, agreement, delivery and account records distinct: identify source, owner, purpose, permissions, stakeholders, need, fit, evidence, stage, value, uncertainty, commitments, decisions and downstream outcome; link corrections and merges without erasing history; and never let an email address, imported row, scored lead or deal card become the customer.
- Treat contactability and permission as different facts: capture each channel, communication purpose, lawful or policy basis where applicable, source, scope, region, timestamp, preference and withdrawal; make current permission visible before outreach and automation; preserve suppression through imports, merges and handoffs; and never infer consent from possession of contact data, a prior transaction, an organizational role or a sales stage.
- Qualify the opportunity around customer evidence rather than seller enthusiasm: preserve the problem or desired outcome, present behavior, fit, constraints, stakeholders, authority, resources, timing, alternatives, risks, decision process, evidence source and next learning step; support disqualification, uncertainty and later re-entry with reasons; and never turn a score, demographic proxy, activity volume or required field into proof of need or readiness.
- Model stakeholders as plural, role-specific and changeable: distinguish user, beneficiary, evaluator, champion, approver, budget owner, procurement, legal, security, implementation and executive roles; record source and confidence without exposing sensitive inference; support accessible communication and customer correction; and never collapse a buying group into one decision maker or treat seniority, title or engagement as authority.
- Keep activity, learning, progress and commitment separate: source-link messages, calls, meetings, notes and generated summaries; distinguish attempted from completed contact and seller action from customer evidence; require a reason and next action for material stage changes; show staleness, regression, slippage and missing information; and never reward activity count or AI summary volume as if it demonstrated customer progress.
- Represent pipeline and forecast fields as inspectable, time-bound judgments: keep target, potential value, expected value, probability, forecast category, close date, stage and commitment distinct; identify source, method, assumptions, confidence, owner and last material change; preserve prior submissions and explain movement; offer drill-through from aggregates; and never let a stage-derived percentage, weighted total or executive target masquerade as customer commitment.
- Carry exact commercial scope from opportunity into proposal while preserving boundaries: identify products or services, quantities, price basis, currency, discounts, taxes, assumptions, exclusions, validity, dependencies, approval authority and customer-facing version; distinguish draft, internally approved, sent, viewed, negotiated, accepted and superseded; link rather than duplicate agreement records; and never treat a generated quote, sent proposal or verbal signal as a signed agreement.
- Make won, lost, disqualified, dormant and no-decision states reasoned and reversible through evidence: capture who made the classification, when, customer-confirmed or inferred reason, alternative chosen, unmet need, commercial outcome, remaining obligations, permission for future contact and appropriate re-entry condition; distinguish close from delivery and realized value; and never use a closed state to erase ambiguity, inflate success or authorize continued pursuit.
- Treat handoff as an accountable transfer of customer context and commitments: carry confirmed need, sold scope, proposal and agreement versions, promises, exclusions, acceptance criteria, stakeholders, channel preferences, dependencies, dates, risks, open questions, implementation and relationship owners; let the customer inspect consequential assumptions; acknowledge receipt and resolve gaps; and never reduce onboarding or delivery to a deal name, amount and internal notes.
- Measure durable customer and operational outcomes rather than seller activity alone: track permission quality, qualification reversals, forecast error and calibration, stage aging and slippage, proposal correction, loss reasons, handoff defects, unmet commitments, onboarding friction, realized value, complaints, accessibility and relationship burden; segment responsibly and protect workers from surveillance incentives; and never optimize call volume, pipeline inflation or close rate at the expense of fit, agency, truthfulness or delivery.

## Variation levers

- Increase permission provenance, identity matching and source quality controls for high-volume inbound, enrichment and outbound acquisition.
- Increase stakeholder, security, procurement, legal and multi-stage approval context for enterprise sales.
- Increase pricing configuration, discount authority, tax, validity and version controls for product-led or transactional proposals.
- Increase scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria and delivery ownership for professional services and implementation-heavy sales.
- Increase realized-value, complaint, support, renewal and expansion evidence for long-lived customer relationships.

## Tensions and tradeoffs

- Fast capture and enrichment versus identity accuracy, privacy and permission.
- Consistent qualification versus customer diversity and contextual judgment.
- Seller productivity versus customer agency and communication burden.
- Simple stages versus nonlinear, multi-stakeholder decisions.
- Forecast comparability versus honest uncertainty and changing evidence.
- Commercial momentum versus truthful scope, approval and delivery readiness.

## Failure modes

- A contact record is treated as communication consent.
- A lead score or filled field becomes qualification evidence.
- Activity volume is reported as customer progress.
- A buying group is collapsed into one decision maker.
- Stage-derived probability becomes forecast certainty.
- A sent quote is treated as customer agreement.
- Won or lost status erases reasons, permission and uncertainty.
- Delivery receives only the deal name, value and seller notes.

## Anti-patterns

- Contact means consent.
- Score means fit.
- Activity theater.
- Single-decision-maker fiction.
- Weighted-pipeline certainty.
- Quote as contract.
- Closed-state amnesia.
- Revenue-wall handoff.

## Acceptance and review questions

- Can one relationship be reconstructed without confusing person, contact point, organization, lead, opportunity, proposal, agreement, delivery or account?
- Is communication permission explicit by channel, purpose, source, scope, region and time, including withdrawal?
- Does qualification preserve customer need, fit, constraints, stakeholders, alternatives, uncertainty and disqualification reasons?
- Are stakeholder roles plural, sourced, correctable and distinct from inferred authority?
- Can people distinguish attempted activity, completed interaction, learning, stage progress and customer commitment?
- Are target, value, probability, forecast category, close date, stage and commitment independently sourced and versioned?
- Does proposal handling preserve exact scope, pricing, assumptions, exclusions, approvals, versions and agreement boundaries?
- Do closed states retain reason, evidence, uncertainty, remaining obligations, contact permission and re-entry conditions?
- Does handoff carry confirmed commitments, exclusions, acceptance, stakeholders, dependencies, risks, dates and owners?
- Do metrics expose forecast quality, handoff defects, unmet commitments, complaints, accessibility and customer burden—not only activity and close rate?
