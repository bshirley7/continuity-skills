# Camera scanning and visual capture

## Generalized principles

- Introduce camera capture as a scoped acquisition method with an equivalent route: explain the exact object and purpose, required sides or pages, downstream use, on-device or remote processing and retention before permission; support gallery, file upload, manual entry, assisted or in-person alternatives where appropriate; and never make camera ownership, vision, dexterity, lighting or bandwidth an unstated eligibility requirement.
- Guide acquisition with actionable multimodal quality feedback: show target boundaries, orientation, page or side, focus, distance, lighting, glare, motion, occlusion and completeness; distinguish guidance from capture and processing state; support automatic and manual shutter, flashlight, zoom and pause where useful; and describe the specific fix instead of repeatedly saying the image failed.
- Preserve the source and every material transform for review: retain page identity, order and side; expose crop, rotation, enhancement, compression, redaction and extracted text as reversible layers; let people compare processed output with the original, add, replace, reorder or recapture one item without restarting, and avoid transformations that erase faint marks, security features, handwriting or context.
- Separate capture, recognition, extraction, authenticity, ownership, eligibility and final outcome: show recognized code, document type, fields or amount before using them; bind extracted values to their source region; flag uncertainty and mismatch; require exact review for names, identifiers, dates, medication, account and monetary values; and never imply that a clear image, OCR result, liveness result or successful upload independently proves a later claim.
- Scale commitment and recovery to consequence: preview the exact pages, fields, recipient, account and action before legal, financial, health, identity or public submission; keep recapture and precise edit routes; distinguish saved draft, uploaded, received, under review, accepted and rejected; preserve a receipt and source-bound correction path; and offer trained human or offline review when automated capture repeatedly fails.
- Govern images, extracted data and metadata as sensitive evidence: minimize field of view and collection, warn about bystanders and unrelated content, strip unnecessary location and device metadata, provide redaction before sharing, protect local previews and temporary files, validate uploads safely, disclose recipients and retention, and support access, export, correction and deletion without destroying required evidentiary lineage.

## Variation levers

- Increase source preservation, explicit field review, human validation and audit lineage with legal, financial, health, identity or safety consequence.
- Use automatic capture for stable bounded documents and manual capture when timing, context or accessibility requires user control.
- Allow enhancement for readability while retaining an unaltered source when authenticity, faint marks, color or security features matter.
- Prefer direct code resolution for low-risk lookup; require destination and consequence review before opening links, joining accounts, authorizing payments or sharing data.

## Tensions and tradeoffs

- Automatic capture reduces motor effort while taking the image before the user understands framing or surrounding content.
- Image enhancement improves readability while removing evidence needed to judge authenticity or meaning.
- OCR accelerates entry while turning uncertain pixels into authoritative-looking fields.
- Liveness and anti-spoof controls reduce fraud while excluding disabled users and increasing biometric collection.
- Keeping originals supports audit and correction while expanding sensitive-data retention and breach impact.

## Failure modes

- Camera permission is requested before purpose, scope or alternatives are explained.
- Quality feedback says try again without identifying glare, blur, cropping, orientation or missing side.
- A scan is auto-enhanced with no original, comparison or undo.
- OCR fills consequential fields that are submitted without source-linked review.
- A successful upload checkmark is styled as identity, eligibility or payment approval.
- One bad page forces the entire document or application to restart.
- Repeated biometric or document failure has no human or non-camera route.
- A QR code opens or commits to an opaque destination immediately after recognition.
- Background people, location metadata or unrelated documents are captured and retained.
- Deleting a local preview appears to delete the submitted evidence or vice versa.

## Anti-patterns

- Permission before purpose.
- Generic recapture loop.
- Irreversible enhancement.
- OCR as truth.
- Upload equals approval.
- Restart for one page.
- Camera-gated exclusion.
- Scan and execute.
- Unbounded field of view.
- Ambiguous deletion.

## Acceptance and review questions

- Is the camera purpose, scope, processing, retention and equivalent non-camera route clear before permission?
- Does quality guidance identify the exact page, side and actionable issue through accessible visual and nonvisual feedback?
- Can people review the unaltered source and reverse crop, rotation, enhancement, redaction, extraction, order and recapture at the right grain?
- Are capture, recognition, extraction, authenticity, ownership, eligibility and final outcome represented as distinct claims with source-linked uncertainty?
- Before consequential submission, can people inspect exact pages, fields, recipient and action and recover through precise correction or human review?
- Are field of view, metadata, bystanders, temporary files, uploads, recipients, retention, export, correction, deletion and evidentiary lineage governed safely?
