# Industry lenses

- **Consumer:** reduce time-to-value, keep recovery obvious, and earn repeat attention without coercive loops.
- **B2B/SaaS:** make system status, permissions, collaboration ownership, and operational consequences explicit.
- **Finance:** foreground trust, reversibility, risk, provenance, and exact monetary consequences.
- **Health:** use calm clarity, accessible language, safe escalation, privacy cues, and uncertainty-aware guidance.
- **Commerce:** keep product comparison, total cost, fulfillment, returns, and purchase confidence legible.
- **Media/social:** balance creation and discovery with authorship, context, moderation, and attention boundaries.
- **Travel:** support comparison under uncertainty, itinerary continuity, disruption recovery, and local context.
- **Education:** show progress and feedback without confusing completion with understanding.
- **Developer/AI:** expose system state, inputs, outputs, limitations, inspection paths, and safe recovery.

These are decision lenses, not visual stereotypes. Apply only what the product context supports.
