# Sections and flows

- **Marketing and conversion:** establish relevance before detail; align the primary action with the promised value and make risk visible.
- **Onboarding:** earn each request for effort or data; sequence setup around an early meaningful outcome.
- **Navigation and dashboards:** prioritize orientation, current state, and next decisions over equal exposure of every capability.
- **Discovery and creation:** keep exploration distinct from commitment; preserve context when moving between finding and making.
- **Collaboration, settings, and permissions:** show ownership, scope, consequences, and recoverability before changes apply.
- **Checkout:** keep total cost, fulfillment, correction, and commitment state continuously legible.
- **Empty and error states:** explain what happened, what remains safe, and the most useful recovery path.
- **Retention:** preserve saved state, progress, ongoing value, renewal terms, plan control, cancellation consequences, and reactivation so return is restoration rather than restart.
- **Corporate website:** establish a stable institutional hierarchy across primary business, stakeholder, regional, and governed disclosure routes.
- **High-consequence decisions:** place current state, affected scope, material consequences, exact commitment, proportional confirmation, and recovery in one inspectable flow.
- **Reminders and re-engagement:** connect interruption to a recognizable goal or changed state; expose purpose, channel, timing, pause, reschedule, disable, and honest progress.
- **Contextual learning:** teach meaningful outcomes in the working context; distinguish required, optional, blocked, skipped, and completed learning while preserving rediscovery.
- **Contextual help and recovery:** keep affected work visible, explain verified state, match recovery to operation safety, and escalate with scoped diagnostic context.
- **Consent and privacy controls:** separate purpose, data, recipient, duration, required processing, optional use, withdrawal, export, deletion, and delegated access into durable, revisitable choices.
- **Authentication and account recovery:** distinguish identity, sign-in method, verification factor, recovery channel, trusted device, session, and provider while preserving secure fallbacks and explicit post-recovery state.
- **Data portability:** expose source, destination, scope, format, access, transformation, mapping, overwrite behavior, durable progress, partial results, correction, rollback, and retained-data state.
- **Bulk operations:** keep exact selection scope, change semantics, dependencies, permanence, durable execution state, partial results, recovery, actor, time, and history inspectable.
- **Activity, audit, and version history:** choose explicitly between collaborative orientation, administrative evidence, and object comparison; preserve actor, action, object, scope, outcome, exact time, present-state context, investigation filters, and safe recovery.
- **Notification centers and inbox triage:** separate notification state from work state; preserve receipt rationale, actor, event, object, scope, urgency, return context, grouping evidence, delivery controls, quiet periods, and preview privacy.
- **Search, filtering, and discovery:** expose scope, matching model, query, facets, sorting, result evidence, provenance, uncertainty, preserved return state, and honest zero-result recovery.
- **Complex forms and review:** structure questions around user reasoning, preserve drafts and valid work, distinguish progress from submission, support targeted review edits, and provide durable completion evidence.
- **Analytical exploration:** make question, scope, measures, filters, comparisons, uncertainty, underlying evidence, saved-query identity, provenance, and correction continuously inspectable.
- **Support case lifecycle:** preserve one durable case across intake, routing, channels, transfers, investigation, remedy, verification, reopening, escalation, and appeal without making the person reconstruct their history.
- **Conversation lifecycle:** keep audience, topic, reply scope, provenance, correction, and handoff legible; promote consequential decisions and commitments into durable linked objects rather than inferring them from conversational signals.
- **Operational exception and incident handling:** distinguish signals, anomalies, exceptions, and incidents; maintain a challengeable situation view, bounded mitigation controls, evidence-backed recovery, and verified learning.
- **Review and approval lifecycle:** bind requests to exact versions, make authority and routing inspectable, support evidence-based decisions, and keep approval distinct from execution, publication, payment, or completion.
- **Policy and rule administration:** express rules as versioned contracts, make case evaluation and precedence legible, govern exceptions, and separate testing, publication, distribution, and enforcement.
- **AI memory and context management:** separate session context, saved memory, instructions, inferences, connected sources, shared knowledge, analytics, and model-improvement data; make active scope, provenance, correction, forgetting, and downstream effects inspectable.
- **Experimentation and staged rollout:** bind hypotheses, variants, assignment, exposure, measures, guardrails, validity diagnostics, decisions, rollout controls, rollback, and post-release learning to exact versions and evidence.
- **Accessible media and multimodal content:** preserve equivalent meaning, structure, timing, controls, and recovery across audio, video, text, captions, transcripts, description, sign language, and assistive technologies.
- **Offline work, synchronization, and conflict recovery:** distinguish local, queued, synchronized, stale, conflicting, and failed state; preserve provenance and user work through interruption, reconnection, reconciliation, and recovery.
- **Speech input and voice interaction:** make listening, recognition, interpretation, turn state, audience, privacy, correction, confirmation, and equivalent non-speech control explicit.
- **Camera scanning and visual capture:** separate acquisition, transformation, recognition, evidence, and decision while preserving source review, accessibility, correction, privacy, and consequence-scaled commitment.
- **File Attachment Lifecycle:** Frame the file request before presenting a picker: name the purpose, destination or affected record, whether submission is required, who can access the result, acceptable alternatives, supported types, count and size limits, expected processing and retention; ask for a file only when its information is necessary; and avoid a generic attachment icon when the person must infer what evidence, format or disclosure is expected.
- **Ratings Reviews Reputation Lifecycle:** Present aggregates as bounded evidence rather than a trust badge: show the review count and relevant population, distribution, recency, version or transaction context and category breakdown; distinguish verified interaction, incentivized, imported, translated or summarized content; avoid excessive precision and unexplained weighting; and do not let a high average hide a tiny sample, polarized distribution, stale history or materially changed offering.
- **Booking Reservation Availability Lifecycle:** Represent availability as a scoped and time-sensitive fact: show the search criteria and freshness, distinguish available, limited, unavailable, tentative, requestable, held and sold states, explain material eligibility or dependency checks, offer nearby dates, times, locations, providers or equivalent resources, and avoid implying that viewing or selecting a slot guarantees inventory until the system has actually reserved it.
- **Returns Exchanges Refunds Lifecycle:** Calculate value restoration transparently: show item amount, quantity, discounts, promotions, tax, original and return shipping, service or restocking charges, used benefits, partial fulfillment, prior credits and remaining installment balance; identify the exact refund destination and expected amount and timing; distinguish estimated from final value pending inspection; explain rounding and allocation across mixed tenders; and never describe store credit as equivalent to cash without explicit choice.
- **Loyalty Rewards Membership Lifecycle:** Represent the program as three linked but distinct systems: an earned-value ledger, a time-bound status qualification contract, and a redemption marketplace; name which balance or progress measure each surface shows; do not imply that nominal points equal cash, that status progress is spendable, or that an available reward is already entitled.
- **Financial Transfer Payment Lifecycle:** Make the transfer route and value reconstructable before commitment: show funding source, available balance or credit implication, amount sent, currency, exchange rate and its validity window, provider and third-party fees, taxes, amount expected by the recipient, rail, speed, availability date, limits and material protections; distinguish estimates from guaranteed values and never describe an instant initiation as guaranteed recipient access.
- **Order Fulfillment Delivery Handoff Lifecycle:** Treat time as a revisable promise with provenance: show the original date or window, current estimate, confidence or range where material, last update, reason for change, dependency and next reassessment; distinguish ship-by, carrier acceptance, arrival, out-for-delivery, pickup readiness and hold deadline; notify meaningful slippage before the promise expires; and offer reschedule, consent-to-delay, cancellation or remedy according to the actual contract.
- **Pregnancy Prenatal Birth Postpartum Lifecycle:** Preserve reproductive autonomy across possible, confirmed, uncertain, ended, adverse-outcome, birth, and postpartum states; keep pregnancy information private, sourced, revisable, and range-based; connect maternal warning signs to qualified care without engagement barriers; stop triggering content after changed outcomes; and continue the person's physical, mental, and longitudinal care independently from infant content.
- **Employment Discovery Application Selection Offer Lifecycle:** Preserve one versioned opportunity from discovery through closure; distinguish recommendation, eligibility, application, assessment, selection, offer, and employment states; keep candidate evidence inspectable and correctable; make assessments and adverse evidence explicit; and issue exact, durable offer and closure records.
- **Housing Discovery Rental Application Lease Residency Lifecycle:** Preserve one verified unit and terms record from discovery through tenancy; make ranking, total cost, application, screening, lease, condition, payment, maintenance, renewal, termination, and move-out states inspectable; and prevent unit substitution, hidden charges, evidence loss, and deadline-driven displacement.
- **Learning Enrollment Assessment Completion Credential Lifecycle:** Preserve one versioned learning offer from discovery through credential; distinguish enrollment, participation, practice, assessment, mastery, completion and credential; keep learner control, accessible participation, evidence, results, records and exact credential claims inspectable.
