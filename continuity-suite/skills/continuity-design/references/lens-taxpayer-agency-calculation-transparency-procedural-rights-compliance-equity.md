# Taxpayer agency, calculation transparency, procedural rights, and compliance equity

## Generalized principles

- Evaluate taxpayer agency across the complete obligation: can the person identify the liable entity, jurisdiction, period, form, due dates, source evidence, preparer, representative, declaration, submission authority, payment, notice, correction, appeal and finality; can they inspect, question, correct, retain and obtain help; and does the system avoid turning convenience, delegation or embedded compliance into invisible loss of control?
- Audit identity and jurisdiction as dated, purpose-bound claims: distinguish identity proof, taxpayer identifier, legal entity, residence, domicile, citizenship, beneficial ownership, source jurisdiction and representative authority; state why each is needed and what it affects; minimize and mask sensitive data; support correction and redress; and never infer tax status from an address, account, nationality or authentication event without the required facts.
- Test source-to-return provenance: every material figure should link to identified records, period, issuer, transaction, currency, import path, transformation, classification, adjustment and reviewer; expose missing, duplicate, stale and conflicting evidence; preserve original documents and edits for the applicable retention period; and never let automation or a summarized digital record erase the evidence needed to support or challenge the return.
- Require classification explainability proportionate to consequence: show material factual inputs, rule period, jurisdiction, threshold, rate, exemption, allocation, professional judgment and uncertainty; distinguish software suggestion from taxpayer fact, preparer judgment and authority decision; show alternatives and downstream effects; and never present a black-box category, residency, nexus or deduction result as legal certainty.
- Audit calculation transparency as a reproducible chain: reveal taxable base, inclusions and exclusions, rates, brackets, deductions, credits, withholding, prior payments, adjustments, currency conversion, rounding, penalties, interest and jurisdiction allocation; show how each changed input alters the result; distinguish estimate, return, assessment and account balance; and never optimize refund salience or apparent precision at the expense of assumptions and correct-amount review.
- Evaluate submission as a legal and administrative state transition: require accessible review, error checking and correction of the exact artifact, a clear declaration and scoped authority; distinguish prepared, authorized, transmitted, received, accepted, rejected, amended and final; expose acknowledgment and rejection evidence; and never describe software validation, queued transmission or authority receipt as correctness, audit approval or finality.
- Audit representation and assistance without displacing the taxpayer: identify what each preparer, agent, employer, platform or intermediary can access, change, certify, file, pay and receive; support revocation, handoff and independent records; surface conflicts and fees; preserve low-cost or public assistance and accessible alternatives; and never let a service provider become the only holder of filing evidence or the unreviewed source of taxpayer declarations.
- Test procedural rights at every notice and correction: identify authority, issue, affected return, proposed facts and amount, deadline, available response, evidence, payment consequence, representation, appeal and finality; support agreement and disagreement without coercion; preserve the original, response, amendment and outcome; and never hide rights behind a generic alert, support ticket or urgency pattern.
- Evaluate compliance equity and administrative burden: measure time, cost, repeated entry, document and identity failures, digital access, language, disability, representation, correction, rejection, penalty and appeal outcomes across taxpayers and business sizes; preserve non-digital or assisted routes where required; distinguish simplification from shifted labor; and never judge success only by electronic completion or revenue collected.
- Audit outcomes beyond filing rate: measure correct-amount comprehension, source completeness, classification reversals, calculation discrepancies, rejected transmissions, late corrections, notice understanding, response success, appeal access, payment misallocation, refund surprise, record portability, privacy incidents and burden by affected group; include abandoned and represented taxpayers; and never use speed, automation or acceptance alone as proof of a fair and trustworthy compliance experience.

## Method

- Trace one exact obligation from registration and source records through classification, calculation, review, declaration, filing, payment, notice, correction, appeal and finality.
- Separate taxpayer, liable entity, dependent, guardian, preparer, representative, employer, marketplace, software provider, intermediary, payment provider and tax authority.
- Reconstruct every material figure from source record through transformations, rules, adjustments and submitted fields.
- Walk individual, low-income, self-employed, small-business, payroll, cross-border, dependent, disabled, multilingual, represented and low-connectivity scenarios.
- Test stale imports, missing forms, duplicate records, jurisdiction change, identity mismatch, unavailable representative, rejected filing, misallocated payment, notice deadline and amended return.
- Compare product status language with the exact administrative evidence and rights available at that state.

## Ethical safeguards

- Do not infer tax status from address, nationality, payment account or device location alone.
- Do not present automated imports, OCR or classifications as verified facts without review.
- Do not describe an estimate, prepared return, transmitted return or accepted return as authority-confirmed correctness.
- Do not submit or certify a return through delegated authority without exact scope and taxpayer review where required.
- Do not collapse filing, payment, refund, assessment and finality.
- Do not hide notice deadlines, challenge rights, representation or appeal behind generic support.
- Do not make digital identity, accessibility, language or connectivity barriers a de facto penalty.
- Do not retain or expose full taxpayer identifiers beyond the stated need.

## Variation levers

- Increase segregation, reconciliation, approval and audit controls for business, payroll and intermediary filing.
- Increase explanation, guided review and public-assistance paths for individual taxpayers.
- Increase multi-jurisdiction, currency, residency, treaty and record-retention controls for cross-border obligations.
- Increase authority, privacy and supported-decision controls for dependents and guardians.
- Increase accessible, multilingual, offline and representative alternatives where digital-only compliance creates exclusion.

## Tensions and tradeoffs

- Simplified guidance versus complete legal and computational context.
- Prepopulation versus stale, mismatched or unverified data.
- Automation versus inspectability and taxpayer responsibility.
- Fraud prevention versus privacy and equitable access.
- Deadline enforcement versus time for correction and representation.
- Embedded compliance versus independent taxpayer records and choice.

## Failure modes

- A profile attribute becomes an unsupported tax-status determination.
- Source evidence is lost behind a total.
- A tax category cannot be explained or corrected.
- A precise estimate is mistaken for an assessed amount.
- An agent's action exceeds visible authority.
- Transmission or acceptance is presented as final correctness.
- A notice omits a response right or deadline.
- Digital completion improves while burden shifts to excluded taxpayers.

## Anti-patterns

- Address-to-tax leap.
- Total without lineage.
- Tax black box.
- Precision-as-certainty.
- Invisible agent authority.
- Accepted-equals-correct.
- Rightless notice.
- Digital burden laundering.

## Acceptance and review questions

- Can taxpayers identify and control the complete obligation, representatives, decisions, records and rights?
- Are identity and jurisdiction claims dated, purpose-bound, minimal, correctable and distinct from authentication?
- Can every material reported value be traced to current source evidence and transformations?
- Are classification facts, rules, uncertainty, alternatives and consequences inspectable?
- Can a person reproduce material calculations and distinguish estimates from authority states?
- Does filing require accessible exact review, correction, declaration and scoped authorization?
- Are taxpayer and representative roles, fees, access, revocation and records explicit?
- Do notices and amendments preserve deadlines, evidence, challenge, appeal and finality?
- Are burden, access, rejection and correction outcomes evaluated across affected groups?
- Do success measures cover correctness, comprehension, rights, privacy and portability beyond filing rate?
