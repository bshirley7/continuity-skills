# Theme controls

- **Editorial:** strong typographic rhythm, deliberate pacing, and content-led composition.
- **Minimal:** fewer simultaneous signals, generous hierarchy, and disciplined exception handling.
- **Expressive:** distinctive form and motion anchored by stable interaction conventions.
- **Premium:** material restraint, precise spacing, controlled contrast, and credible detail.
- **Playful:** warmth, surprise, and forgiving feedback without obscuring task state.
- **Calm:** reduced urgency, predictable motion, quiet surfaces, and reassuring recovery.
- **Data-dense:** compact comparison, persistent context, and layered detail without visual flattening.
- **Technical:** explicit structure, state, provenance, and inspectable system behavior.
- **Cinematic:** narrative staging, depth, and motion with accessibility and performance escape hatches.
- **Utilitarian:** direct language, efficient controls, visible state, and minimal ornamental interference.

Themes control emphasis and expression; they do not override usability or accessibility.
