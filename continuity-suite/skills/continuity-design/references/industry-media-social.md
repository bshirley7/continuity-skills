# Media and social

## Generalized principles

- Distinguish followed, searched, saved, editorial, sponsored, trending, and recommended content, exposing enough source and rationale for users to understand why an item entered the feed.
- Make feed agency granular and reversible through prioritize, follow, mute, show less, hide, block, report, save, and undo, preserving the exact author, topic, community, or recommendation signal affected.
- Keep creation as a recoverable draft and separate preview, metadata, destination, audience, privacy, participation, reuse, schedule, moderation, and external distribution before publication.
- Name publication reach and participation consequences concretely so viewers, commenters, reusers, community members, and external destinations are not collapsed into one public state.
- Organize community attention by object and obligation: separate direct messages, mentions, threads, unread channels, saved items, scheduled reminders, live activity, and general feed updates.
- Preserve the conversation object, channel or community, sender, time, unread boundary, attachment, moderation state, and notification consequence while users act on messages.
- Make creator economics reconstructable through audience source, active and paid members, trials, conversions, cancellations, tier changes, revenue, payout state, and the content or offer that produced each result.
- Keep growth prompts subordinate to current audience, content, safety, and payout state so monetization advice does not masquerade as operational truth.

## Variation levers

- Use an explicit followed feed when subscription intent matters and explained recommendations when discovery matters.
- Use per-item controls for local corrections and preference settings for durable ranking changes.
- Use draft plus preview for broad publication and lightweight send for bounded private conversation.
- Use inbox sections for obligations and a chronological channel for shared context.
- Use cohort and lifecycle views for subscriptions and per-content views for creative diagnosis.
- Use reversible hide for preference correction and report or block for safety boundaries.

## Tensions and tradeoffs

- Recommendation improves discovery while obscuring editorial and ranking agency.
- More feed controls improve agency while increasing menu complexity.
- Rich publishing controls protect creators while slowing expression.
- Unified community surfaces increase continuity while mixing obligations and ambient activity.
- Creator analytics improve strategy while encouraging metric fixation.
- Monetization guidance supports livelihood while pressuring creators toward platform incentives.

## Failure modes

- Recommended content has no visible source or rationale.
- Hide, mute, block, and report are conflated.
- A preference action offers no confirmation or undo.
- Publication lacks draft, preview, audience, participation, or schedule review.
- Public labels do not explain actual reach or reuse.
- Mentions, threads, unread items, and general activity share one undifferentiated count.
- Revenue appears without audience source, lifecycle, content, or payout state.
- Growth prompts outrank safety, moderation, or current creator obligations.

## Anti-patterns

- Calling every ranked item recommended for you.
- Using one not interested action for topic, author, and safety issues.
- Publishing directly from an editor.
- Using public without naming participation and distribution.
- Treating every unread event as equally urgent.
- Flattening direct messages and community broadcasts.
- Showing gross revenue without membership or payout context.
- Presenting platform upsell as creator performance advice.

## Acceptance and review questions

- Can users identify why each feed item appeared?
- Are prioritize, follow, mute, hide, block, report, save, and undo distinct?
- Are draft, preview, destination, audience, privacy, participation, reuse, schedule, and distribution reviewed?
- Does publication language explain actual reach and downstream use?
- Are direct messages, mentions, threads, unread channels, saved items, and ambient updates distinct?
- Do message actions preserve object, sender, time, boundary, attachment, and moderation state?
- Can creator results be traced to audience source, lifecycle, content, revenue, and payout?
- Do safety and current obligations outrank growth prompts?
