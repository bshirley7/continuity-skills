# Generative concept laboratory

Use this laboratory after reference synthesis and before authored directions. Its purpose is to enlarge the search space while experiments are cheap. It is separate from the AI-slop gate: the laboratory proves breadth and lineage; the slop gate critiques execution and drift.

## Operating sequence

1. Derive four to eight lenses from product truth, audience tension, subject matter, material, behavior, and the supplied references' transferable relationships. Do not use style labels alone as lenses.
2. Declare a range plan before making seeds. Cover at least four materially different art-direction families, three typography strategies from three families, three composition families, four page grammars, three interaction or motion strategies, and five page-depth roles. The page roles must include opening, proof, closure, and a quiet or edge state. Record any intentional convergence; shared product truth is not permission for shared visual identity.
   - Typography is a rendered hypothesis, not a font-family label. Test real copy at display, text, and narrow measures; retain source and license evidence; state project-specific customization. At least one study must use a project-supplied, open-licensed, licensed, custom-lettered, or code-native source rather than a system-only stack.
   - Page grammar describes how the whole experience behaves: longform narrative, single-canvas instrument, navigable artifact, editorial issue, cinematic sequence, modular system, or spatial journey. A new color or section order is not a new grammar.
   - Interaction and motion must have semantic purpose, a reduced-motion behavior, a static fallback, and a cheap hash-bound prototype. A multi-concept UI set cannot make every strategy static.
3. Produce eight to twelve inexpensive seeds across the lenses and the range plan. Prefer at least three media. When image generation is available, use it for ideation as well as illustration. Valid seed media include image generation, image editing, SVG, typography, collage, motion frames, and code sketches. Do not let every generated study become abstract tactile sculpture: test documentary, illustrative, typographic, spatial, and interaction-led propositions when the subject supports them.
4. Keep seeds upstream of website composition. Ask each experiment to discover a hierarchy, material behavior, image logic, spatial model, type behavior, motion relationship, narrative device, or deeper-page behavior—not a polished generic landing-page screenshot.
5. Bind every seed to one planned art-direction family, typography strategy, composition family, and one or more page-depth roles. Record the hypothesis, project specificity, surprising quality, transferable mechanics, risk, provenance, and exact artifact hash. Generated output is illustrative evidence, never an observed product fact.
6. Cross-pollinate at least two pairs of seeds from different media or lenses. Use counterfactual prompts such as: what if the imagery supplies navigation, the timeline becomes typography, or authority changes the composition rather than appearing as a badge?
7. For every promising generated art family, produce two to four style-frame variants before selection. A single generated image is evidence of one prompt, not a design system. Record which frames were selected and rejected, why, and at least three system extractions. Use image generation to ask alternate compositional and cultural questions, not just to decorate a chosen layout.
8. Shortlist three to six seeds. Extract relationships rather than copying pixels. When a concept inherits image-generation or image-editing work, record at least three non-image system decisions—cropping, composition, type behavior, spatial rhythm, token semantics, interaction, motion, or responsive transformation—and bind a derived SVG, CSS, HTML, or other first-party artifact. Generated pixels remain private exploration unless separate rights and asset promotion rules permit their use.
9. Cluster the shortlist into one to three concept directions. Only then build comparable responsive HTML or other target-native boards.
10. Before expanding those boards, rebuild the source-defining reference mechanics and the candidate adaptations through [reference translation fidelity](reference-translation-fidelity.md). A generated or referenced material proposition may not collapse into primitive code decoration merely because HTML composition has begun.

## Creative-range gate

Before `concept-validate`, review range independently from AI slop:

- Write a unique five-second reaction for every concept.
- Compare every concept pair across organizing idea, primary carrier, emotional register, material system, typography, imagery, motion, voice, and responsive transformation. Every pair must differ materially in at least three dimensions.
- Run an adversarial pass for every concept: make the strongest case that it is generic, propose an opposing hypothesis, change the work in response, and record what was rejected.
- Check recurring Continuity house tells explicitly, including restrained serif-plus-mono typography, thin rules, quiet neutral fields, orange interruptions, proof rails, and editorial evidence ledgers. These devices may be used only when project identity—not habit—demands them.
- Require a unique typography strategy, art-direction family, and composition family for every concept in a multi-concept set, with at least two typography families overall. This is a range floor, not an instruction to maximize difference after evidence favors convergence.
- Require a unique planned page grammar and interaction or motion strategy for every concept. Test page architecture before the browser turns every proposition into a familiar vertical marketing stack.
- Map each concept through at least five journey stages including opening, proof, closure, and a quiet or edge state. Confirm that the signature changes appropriately through the journey instead of repeating one hero treatment.
- Make the signature materially control at least three different journey roles. A recognizable hero with generic proof, edge, and closure states is not a compelling concept.
- Provide a tall full-page strip, an exact journey index, and a deep link to each complete prototype. A comparison page showing only hero-sized windows cannot prove depth.
- Capture mobile, tablet, and desktop runtime probes for each concept itself. Comparison-page probes do not prove that an individual concept transforms correctly.
- Confirm that the signature survives outside the hero and that the project could generate a family of surfaces without cloning one composition.
- Run a separate multimodal impact review. At least one direction must be judged `compelling`, not merely valid: identifiable, emotionally resonant, coherent, and visibly project-specific in both wide and narrow evidence. Record the weakest moment and next refinement priority for every concept.
- Bind a portfolio review at least every four generations. Compare eight fingerprint dimensions, disposition any recurring tell retained by a concept, and challenge the strongest prior generation with current wide and narrow evidence.
- Require at least one media system to be `concept-forming`: the media must change the thesis, composition, and behavior. Supporting atmosphere does not satisfy this requirement.
- Give every concept a hash-bound purposeful-motion storyboard or an explicit no-motion storyboard, then prove the signature through at least four downstream roles beyond opening.

Run `continuity design concept-lab-validate --input <laboratory.json>` before drafting directions. A completed mixed-media laboratory requires the range plan, four to eight lenses, eight to twelve unique hashed seed artifacts, at least three media including image generation or editing, at least two cross-pollinations, two or more style-frame families with selected and rejected variants, and three to six shortlisted seeds. Offline code-only exploration remains valid. If the laboratory is unavailable, declined, or deliberately omitted, record that explicitly with a rationale; expressive benchmark work cannot use the omission path.
