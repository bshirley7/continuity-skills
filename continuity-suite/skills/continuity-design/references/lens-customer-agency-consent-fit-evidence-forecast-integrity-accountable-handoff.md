# Customer agency, consent, fit evidence, forecast integrity, and accountable handoff

## Generalized principles

- Audit relationship identity and purpose across the full lifecycle: can people distinguish person, contact point, organization, lead, opportunity, proposal, agreement, delivery and account; inspect source, owner, purpose, permissions, stakeholders, need, evidence, versions and corrections; prevent silent duplicate merges and cross-purpose reuse; and avoid treating an imported record, enriched profile or deal object as the customer?
- Evaluate communication permission in its applicable legal and relationship context: preserve channel, purpose, source, scope, region, timestamp, sender responsibility, preference and withdrawal; distinguish marketing from transactional communication and consent from other applicable bases; carry suppression through vendors, imports and handoffs; and never generalize one jurisdiction's rule or infer permission from possession of contact data.
- Test qualification for customer need, fit and non-exploitation: require sourced evidence about desired outcome, present behavior, constraints, stakeholders, resources, timing, alternatives, risks and decision process; show uncertainty and allow disqualification or re-entry; avoid vulnerable-trait targeting and demographic proxies; and never let a score, title, activity level or seller target substitute for customer-defined value.
- Audit persuasion and commercial disclosure for truthful, timely and accessible choice: identify the seller and offer, state material claims, scope, price, fees, assumptions, exclusions, validity and consequences before commitment, make alternatives and refusal usable, provide review and correction, and never use false urgency, hidden terms, misleading hierarchy, forced disclosure or repeated pressure to manufacture progress.
- Evaluate stakeholder representation for plurality, accessibility and real authority: distinguish affected user, beneficiary, evaluator, champion, approver, budget owner, procurement, legal, security and implementation roles; source and time-bound claims about influence; support accessible participation and correction; and never collapse a group into one decision maker or infer authority from seniority, title, engagement or an algorithm.
- Test scoring, summaries and recommendations as bounded decision support: expose inputs, provenance, purpose, method, confidence, known limitations, affected decision and human owner; separate observed activity from inferred intent and customer evidence; enable correction, override and audit; monitor disparate and downstream effects; and never let automated enrichment or generation silently create qualification, authority, probability or permission.
- Audit forecasts as accountable, versioned estimates rather than performance theater: distinguish target, potential value, expected value, probability, forecast category, close date, stage and customer commitment; document source, assumptions, method, confidence, owner and overrides; preserve prior submissions and explain slippage; reconcile aggregates to records; and never let stage defaults, compensation pressure or executive targets rewrite uncertainty.
- Evaluate proposal, close and value states without category error: preserve exact offer version, internal approval, customer acceptance, agreement, promised performance, delivery evidence, complaint and outcome as separate states; apply financial-reporting standards only in their proper context; let losses and no-decisions retain reasons and uncertainty; and never present a sent proposal, signature, closed-won label, invoice or booked amount as fulfilled customer value or recognized revenue by itself.
- Audit handoff as a confirmed transfer of obligations and relationship context: carry the approved proposal and agreement versions, customer-defined need, promises, exclusions, acceptance criteria, stakeholders, permissions, dependencies, dates, risks, open questions and accountable owners; minimize unrelated private notes; let recipients and the customer identify gaps; preserve acknowledgement and correction; and never substitute seller shorthand for delivery-ready commitments.
- Evaluate remedy, exit and metrics around durable customer outcomes: make opt-out, correction, complaint, loss, no-decision, cancellation and re-entry understandable and accessible; preserve reasons without retaliation; measure permission failures, forecast calibration, correction loops, handoff defects, unmet commitments, realized value, complaints, accessibility and burden; and never optimize activity, pipeline or close rate by hiding customer refusal, friction or downstream harm.

## Method

- Trace one exact customer relationship from source and contact permission through qualification, opportunity, forecast, proposal, close, delivery handoff, realized outcome, complaint, renewal or exit.
- Separate customer, contact point, organization, beneficiary, user, evaluator, champion, approver, budget owner, seller, manager, proposal approver, implementation owner and independent assurance roles.
- Reconstruct every permission, fit, stage, probability, forecast, price, claim, close and handoff judgment from source evidence, jurisdiction or policy, method, version, authority and current uncertainty.
- Walk inbound, imported, enriched, referred, outbound, opted-out, duplicate, inaccessible, multi-stakeholder, long-cycle, regulated, lost, no-decision, won-but-not-delivered and complaint scenarios.
- Test mistaken identity, withdrawn permission, unsupported score, AI-generated summary, stale opportunity, pressured close, hidden term, changed proposal, forecast override, duplicate record and incomplete handoff.
- Compare activity and revenue metrics with permission quality, forecast calibration, qualification reversal, accessibility, complaints, handoff defects, unmet commitments, realized value and customer burden.

## Ethical safeguards

- Do not treat contact data, public availability, employment role, prior transaction or organizational relationship as universal permission to communicate.
- Do not infer sensitive traits, vulnerability, authority, budget, intent or readiness from demographic proxies, enrichment, surveillance or opaque scoring.
- Do not use urgency, scarcity, hidden terms, confirmshaming, repeated interruption or asymmetric cancellation to override customer choice.
- Do not make a lead score, activity count, stage, probability, forecast category or AI summary an unreviewable decision or factual claim.
- Do not collapse a buying group into one stereotyped decision maker or exclude people through inaccessible communication and review.
- Do not let targets, compensation or managerial pressure rewrite customer evidence, uncertainty, pricing, scope, loss reasons or forecast history.
- Do not represent a quote, proposal, signature, closed-won state, invoice or booked amount as delivered value or recognized revenue by itself.
- Do not hand downstream teams private, irrelevant or unverified sales notes in place of customer-confirmed commitments and permission-aware context.

## Variation levers

- Increase channel, sender and jurisdiction controls for high-volume outreach and multinational marketing.
- Increase model documentation, human review and disparate-impact testing for automated enrichment, scoring and forecasting.
- Increase stakeholder, procurement, security, legal and accessibility controls for enterprise and regulated buying groups.
- Increase pricing, disclosure, cooling-off, confirmation and complaint safeguards for consumer and self-service sales.
- Increase commitment, acceptance, dependency, outcome and complaint evidence for services and implementation-heavy relationships.

## Tensions and tradeoffs

- Sales efficiency versus customer permission, privacy and attention.
- Consistent qualification versus contextual need and equitable access.
- Personalization versus inference, manipulation and sensitive targeting.
- Forecast comparability versus uncertainty and honest revision.
- Commercial momentum versus accessible disclosure and deliberation.
- Internal note richness versus customer accuracy, dignity and downstream minimization.

## Failure modes

- Contactability is presented as permission.
- A score or title is treated as fit and authority.
- Persuasive friction manufactures consent or urgency.
- AI output silently becomes customer evidence.
- Targets or stage defaults become forecast truth.
- Closed-won, invoice or signature becomes delivered value.
- Downstream teams inherit unverified promises and private notes.
- Opt-out, complaint and loss reasons disappear from performance metrics.

## Anti-patterns

- Reachable means willing.
- Lead-score personhood.
- Pressure as progress.
- Generated customer truth.
- Forecast by quota.
- Booked means delivered.
- Promise dump.
- Refusal erasure.

## Acceptance and review questions

- Can one relationship be reconstructed without conflating identity, contact point, organization, lead, opportunity, proposal, agreement, delivery or account?
- Is permission explicit and applicable by channel, purpose, sender, source, scope, region and time, including withdrawal and suppression?
- Does qualification reflect customer-defined need, fit, constraints, alternatives and uncertainty without proxy-based exploitation?
- Are claims, price, fees, assumptions, exclusions, refusal and consequences clear before commitment without manipulative choice architecture?
- Are stakeholder roles plural, sourced, accessible and distinct from inferred authority?
- Can people inspect and correct the inputs, provenance, limits and owner of scores, summaries and recommendations?
- Are target, probability, category, stage, close date and commitment distinct, versioned and supported by explainable forecast evidence?
- Are proposal, agreement, close, performance, delivery, outcome and revenue states kept distinct within their proper domains?
- Does handoff carry exact approved commitments, permissions, exclusions, acceptance, dependencies, risks, dates and accountable owners?
- Do remedy and metrics expose opt-out, complaint, loss, forecast error, handoff defects, unmet commitments, accessibility and customer burden?
